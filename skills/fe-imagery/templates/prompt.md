# Prompt recipe

Fill every line. Leave a line out only when it truly does not apply.

```text
Subject: <one thing, concretely: "an outrigger boat at anchor", not "travel">
Setting: <where, time of day, weather>
Light: <direction and quality: "low sun from the left, long soft shadows">
Medium: <photograph with lens and aperture, or the illustration style and its line weight>
Palette: <two or three hex colors from the tokens, and which one dominates>
Composition: <ratio, where the subject sits, and the empty area kept for text: "subject in the right third, calm sky in the left half">
Leave out: <text, logos, watermarks, people's faces, extra hands, borders>
```

For a set (cards, empty states), write the Medium, Palette and Light lines once and reuse them word for word, so the set looks like one hand made it.

Sizes to ask for: hero 16:9 or 3:2, card 4:3, portrait 4:5, social card 1200x630, icon source 1:1.
