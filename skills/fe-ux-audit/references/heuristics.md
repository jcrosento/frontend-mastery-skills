# Second pass: the ten heuristics, as questions

Go through the task screen by screen. One finding per line. If you cannot point at an element, it is not a finding.

Severity: 0 not a problem, 1 cosmetic, 2 minor, 3 major (people will get it wrong), 4 blocks the task. Weigh how often it happens, how hard it is to get past, and whether it keeps happening.

| # | Heuristic | Ask on every screen |
|---|-----------|---------------------|
| H1 | Visibility of system status | After a tap, does something change within a second? Is a save, an upload or a wait shown? Does the person know where they are? |
| H2 | Match with the real world | Are the words the ones staff say out loud? Any code, id or internal term on screen? Are money, dates and names shown the way people write them? |
| H3 | User control and freedom | Is there a way back from every screen? Can a wrong tap be undone? Does closing a form lose typed work without warning? |
| H4 | Consistency and standards | Does the same word mean the same thing everywhere? Is the main button in the same place and color on every screen? Does it behave like the apps people already use? |
| H5 | Error prevention | Does the right keyboard open for numbers, email and phone? Are impossible choices disabled, with the reason? Is a destructive action confirmed? |
| H6 | Recognition, not recall | Does the person have to remember something from an earlier screen? Are choices shown instead of typed? Are recent items offered? |
| H7 | Flexibility and efficiency | How many steps and decisions for the daily task? What can be prefilled, defaulted or skipped? Is the main action in thumb reach? |
| H8 | Aesthetic and minimalist | Is there one main thing per screen? What on the screen is never used for this task? Is anything shown twice? |
| H9 | Recovering from errors | Does each error say what happened, in plain words, beside the field, with the next action? Is typed data kept? |
| H10 | Help | Does an empty screen say what goes here and how to add the first one? Is help beside the hard field, not on another page? |

Also read every word once: button labels are verbs, no two buttons say the same thing, no sentence a staff member would have to ask about.

The method and the scale follow https://www.nngroup.com/articles/how-to-conduct-a-heuristic-evaluation/ .
